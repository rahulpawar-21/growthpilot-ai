# 🚀 GrowthPilot AI

An AI-powered business growth platform that helps businesses analyze customers, identify growth opportunities, create personalized marketing messages, and generate actionable growth strategies.

## 🎯 Problem

Small businesses often have customer data but lack the time and tools to understand it and turn it into useful growth actions.

## 💡 Solution

GrowthPilot AI uses AI to transform business and customer information into practical recommendations.

### Key Features

- 🤖 AI Growth Strategy
- 🧠 Autonomous Growth Agent
- 🎯 Customer Opportunity Analysis
- 📊 AI Customer Insights
- ✍️ Personalized Marketing Messages
- 📅 7-Day AI Growth Plan
